Questions

The short answers.

Is it really free?

Yes. The Foundations course, Think Three Moves Ahead, is free: all 10 lessons, the final assessment and the certificate. You don't need a card. Gambit has eight more courses at £9.89 each if you want to go further, but you never have to buy one.

Is this a negotiation course?

It's the game theory underneath every negotiation: threats, bluffs, first moves and deals that repeat. It's free and it's where to start. If you want to go further, Gambit's paid negotiation courses cover bargaining power, the psychology of the other side and how to prepare for a big negotiation.

How long does it take?

About two hours in total. Each lesson takes 8 to 14 minutes, so you can do one at a time whenever you like. Your progress is saved in this browser.

Do I need to be good at maths?

No. If you can add up and compare two numbers, you can do every lesson. A payoff grid is just a small table, like the split or steal one above, and the lessons walk you through each one.

What's the certificate?

Pass the final assessment (7 out of 10) and you get a Gambit certificate in your name, with its own ID and a public link anyone can use to check it. You can download it as a PDF or add it to your LinkedIn profile.

What happens to my email?

We use your name and email to sign you in and put your name on your certificate. We only send course news if you tick the box, and you can unsubscribe any time. See our privacy notice.
