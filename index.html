Why game theory

Every deal is a game, whether you treat it as one or not.

Your best move in a tender, a pricing call or a negotiation depends on what the other side will do, and their best move depends on you. Most professionals learn this the expensive way, one lost margin at a time.

Gambit gives you those reps without the cost. Each course is built by someone who has played the game for real, working alongside academic game theorists, so the theory always comes with the scar tissue.

The method

Play the game first. Learn its name after.

Lectures explain equilibria. Gambit puts you inside one, in 3D lessons and live games against strategy bots, then shows you where it was.

1

Play

Each concept opens as something you do: a 3D scene with Max and Libby, a sealed-bid auction, a supplier standoff. You make the call before anyone tells you the answer.

2

Debrief

Every answer gets instant feedback. Max and Libby replay what happened, mark the equilibrium and show why the tempting move backfired.

3

Apply

You test the idea on scenarios from real buying and selling work, then prove it in the final assessment and earn your certificate.
