# Gambit's characters: Max and Libby

Two hosts appear in every 3D lesson. They explain the idea in conversation and play whatever roles the game needs (robbers, café owners, bidders, a buyer and a supplier). The same two faces through all nine courses give learners someone to follow, and make the courses recognisably Gambit's.

Built in code (no image files): `assets/world/characters.js`. Portraits for the guide panel are rendered from the same models at page load.

## Max

- **Name:** short for *maximise*.
- **Personality:** quick, cheerful and competitive. He grabs the biggest number in front of him, right now. He's usually the first to fall for a trap, which is how the trap gets explained.
- **Look:** green hoodie with a front pocket and white drawstrings, dark denim, white trainers with red soles, dark brown quiff.
- **Colour in lessons:** green (#3F7D5C). His stacks, labels and portrait ring are green.
- **Voice:** short and excited. "Walk free? Now that's a big number."

## Libby

- **Name:** short for *equilibrium*.
- **Personality:** calm, curious and a step ahead. She asks where a game ends up once everyone has made their best move. She names the concept ("That's a dominant strategy") and never makes Max look stupid.
- **Look:** mustard jacket over a navy and cream striped top, round glasses, a bob with two space buns tied in gold.
- **Colour in lessons:** gold (#D6A33E).
- **Voice:** plain and precise. "Same sheet in my room. Read the small print, Max."

## Rules for writing them

- **One idea per line:** each step has two or three lines. Max reacts, Libby explains, or the other way round.
- **Libby defines each new term,** in bold, the first time it appears.
- **Max makes the tempting mistake** before the learner is asked the question, so the learner gets to beat him to the answer.
- **Gambit vocabulary only:** see `training-library/courses/GLOSSARY.md`. No real companies or clients.
- **They're friends.** Even when the game pits them against each other, they tease and never sneer.

## Extras

Side characters use the same body with a different outfit, such as the detective's trench coat, fedora and moustache. Keep extras in muted colours so Max and Libby stay the brightest things in the scene.
